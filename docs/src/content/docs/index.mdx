## What is PhenoMe?

**PhenoMe** is a reproducible phenotyping pipeline built around three ideas:

- **Embeddings as the common representation.** Any vision model that can describe images can be plugged in; all downstream analyses work on the resulting digital descriptions (vectors).
- **Metadata (experimental context) as the extra info about your images.** Tell the pipeline about conditions, doses, time points, plates, and wells.
- **Classical properties to keep results easy to understand.** Shape, intensity, texture, and quality metrics are computed alongside the embeddings and can be linked back to them to explain what the AI sees.

## How it fits together

![Pipeline Overview](../../assets/pipeline_overview.png)

PhenoMe follows a modular 4-step workflow:

1.  **Discover** — Identifies your raw microscopy images and masks.
2.  **Embeddings** (A) — Handles preprocessing and runs the vision model to extract **visual fingerprints** (digital descriptions of each image).
3.  **Properties** (B) — Extracts explainable **classical features** (like size, brightness, and texture).
4.  **Analyze** (C) — Merges both branches for comparing images, visualizing patterns (PCA/t-SNE/UMAP), interactive plotting, and reporting.

The same four steps underpin every workflow. Everything else (interactive exploration, extension hooks, etc.) plugs into that backbone.
